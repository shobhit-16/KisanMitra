    TIP OF THE DAY
    Excessive Urea Use: Silent Killer of Your Soil
    Using more than 2 bags of urea per acre doesn't make your crop grow faster — it makes soil acidic and kills earthworms. The extra nitrogen evaporates as ammonia (you smell it!) or leaches into groundwater. Apply in 2-3 split doses instead of one heavy dose.
