# Indian Agriculture Digital Product — User Brief

## Vision
Design a high-impact digital product for Indian agriculture that addresses systemic challenges faced by smallholder farmers.

## Core Challenges to Address

### 1. Smallholder Dominance & Income Volatility
- Indian agriculture dominated by small & marginal farmers (85%+ of holdings, <2 hectares)
- Limited capital, storage, and market access
- Income highly vulnerable to price volatility and policy shocks (export bans, MSP changes)
- Distress sales immediately after harvest due to poverty and cash needs
- Low bargaining power in output markets

### 2. Soil Degradation & Unbalanced Input Use
- soils degrading, NPK ratio skewed due to fertilizer over-dependence
- Crops grown not suitable for geography (e.g., water-intensive in water-scarce regions)
- Declining fertilizer response rates → higher costs, lower productivity
- Farmers lack crop diversification information; follow high-MSP crops (rice, wheat)
- Displacement of traditional crops (millets) impacts national nutrition
- India is highly protein deficient partly due to displaced traditional crops

### 3. Value Chain & Information Fragmentation
- Information highly siloed: soil health cards, mandi prices, government schemes
- Multiple portals, fragmented access
- Farmers struggle to access, interpret, and combine information for decisions
- Poor decision-making on crops, inputs, risk management, marketing

### 4. Climate Risk
- Yield risks due to climate change increasingly severe
- Climate action plans at district level rarely reach farmers
- Climate-resilient practices not personalized or actionable at farm level

### 5. Government Schemes & Digital Infrastructure Gaps
- Schemes: Soil Health Card, PM KISAN, Agristack, FPO promotion
- Access uneven, benefits unclear at farmer level
- Digital infrastructure exists but not reaching intended beneficiaries effectively

## Desired Outcomes
- Improve farmer income stability and bargaining power
- Promote sustainable input practices and crop diversification
- Aggregate fragmented information into actionable insights
- Personalize climate risk guidance
- Bridge gap between government schemes and farmer access

## Known Constraints
- Low digital literacy in rural India
- Language diversity
- Connectivity issues in remote areas
- Limited smartphone penetration among marginal farmers

## Research Direction
Draw from:
- Research papers on Indian agriculture
- Multilateral institution reports (World Bank, FAO, IFPRI, RBI)
- Government data (Ministry of Agriculture, NITI Aayog, ICAR)
- Ground-level insights on farm realities
