## Project Notes

### indian-agri-digital-product

**Vision**: B2B2F digital agriculture platform for India targeting smallholder farmers

**Phase**: 01-analysis complete

**Key Findings**:
1. Information is NOT the binding constraint — structural issues (credit, storage, MSP) dominate
2. Viable in narrow band — climate advisory for irrigated commercial farmers via B2B2F
3. Voice-first, offline-capable delivery required (30-45% smartphone, 30% digital literacy)
4. B2B2F model fragile — institution interests don't always align with farmer welfare

**Critical Gaps Identified**:
- Intermediary economics not deeply researched
- Women farmers invisible in analysis
- WhatsApp dependency is strategic risk
- Offline-first architecture incomplete
- Government API integration assumed unreliable

**Specs Created**: 9 domain spec files covering farmer model, climate, market, soil, schemes, channels, institution integration, identity

**Journal Entries**:
- 0001: Information paradox is a red herring
- 0002: Intermediary economics GAP
- 0003: Buyer-user mismatch is structural
- 0004: Women farmers GAP
- 0005: Offline-first and WhatsApp are contradictory

**Major Findings (Phase 01)**:
- Information paradox: digital can't fix analog problems (credit, storage, MSP infrastructure)
- 3 engines COMPETE for farmer resources, not complement
- Health shocks are primary distress driver, not price timing
- Women farmers need fundamentally different product (SHG-based, not individual)
- Intermediaries will resist; make them irrelevant, not partners
- Weather/plots data insufficient for true personalization
- Viable path: Climate first → Income → Soil (sequenced)

**Key Blind Spots Identified**:
- Health shock → distress sale cascade
- Labor constraints as structural (not cost)
- Caste/social network constraints on decisions
- Food security before market optimization
- Mental health and decision fatigue
- Local political economy

**Red Team Verdict**: NOT viable as described. Viable in narrower form with sequencing.
