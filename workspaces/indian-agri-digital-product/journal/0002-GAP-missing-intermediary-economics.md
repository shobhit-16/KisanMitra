# GAP: Intermediary Economics Are Under-researched

## Finding

The analysis identifies input dealers and arthiyas as key intermediaries but does NOT deeply research:
1. How these intermediaries actually make money
2. What would make them share information vs hoard it
3. Whether they can become product channel partners vs remain obstacles

### Why This Is a Gap
The B2B2F model assumes we can work WITH intermediaries (make them channel partners) rather than against them. But:
- Arthiyas profit from information asymmetry — why would they share prices?
- Input dealers earn 10-30% margin on fertilizers — why would they recommend reduced input use?
- The product concept doesn't explain how to make intermediariesincentivized to change behavior

### What We Need to Know
- Actual dealer margin structures by product
- Case studies of dealer networks that shifted to advisory role (e.g., IPL, Corteva distributor networks)
- Farmer trust in dealers vs distrust of government/technology
- Whether digital intermediaries (WhatsApp, YouTube) are displacing dealer advisory role

### Action Required
Follow-up research on intermediary economics before finalizing B2B2F channel strategy.
