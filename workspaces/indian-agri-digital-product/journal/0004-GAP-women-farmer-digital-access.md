# GAP: Women Farmers Are Invisible in the Analysis

## Finding

The analysis mentions "women farmers face additional barriers" but does NOT deeply research:
1. Decision-making patterns of women in agricultural households
2. What digital access barriers women specifically face (vs male household members)
3. Whether women can be reached directly or only through male family members
4. What women-specific schemes exist and why they aren't accessed

### Why This Matters

India has ~65 million women farmers (as per 2019 NARI census) — making up a significant portion of the agricultural workforce. Yet:
- Land records typically in male name
- Mobile phone ownership lower for women
- Social barriers to visiting government offices or markets
- Women's contribution systematically undercounted in agricultural statistics

### Product Implications

If women farmers are systematically excluded:
- The product reaches male farmers only
- Women's labor is uncompensated in the data
- Crop decisions that women influence aren't captured
- Scheme access remains broken for women

### Research Needed

1. What % of cultivated land has women as primary decision-maker?
2. Mobile ownership and usage patterns for rural women
3. Successful models for reaching women farmers (Mahila Doodh Dairy? Self-help groups?)
4. Women-specific scheme utilization (Kisan Credit Card for women?)
