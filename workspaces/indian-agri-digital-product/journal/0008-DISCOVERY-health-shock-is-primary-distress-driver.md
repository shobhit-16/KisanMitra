# DISCOVERY: Health Shocks Are the Primary Driver of Distress Sales, Not Price Timing

## The Finding

The Income Engine (Engine 3) is built on the assumption that distress sales happen because:
- Farmers lack price information
- They sell at harvest when prices are low
- Storage could capture 15-20% price appreciation

**This is the wrong problem.**

Research shows the PRIMARY driver of distress sales is **acute household health crises**, not price timing.

---

## The Mechanism

```
Health shock (illness, accident, hospitalization)
         ↓
Immediate cash need (within days)
         ↓
No savings → No credit access → Can't wait for better price
         ↓
Sell immediately at whatever price offered
         ↓
Seasonal income loss → Next season can't afford inputs
         ↓
Borrow from moneylender at high rate → Debt cycle
```

**The price at harvest is IRRELEVANT to this farmer.** They would sell at the same price regardless of market timing because they need cash NOW.

---

## Why This Matters for the Income Engine

The Income Engine recommends:
- "Store grain for 2 months to capture ₹800/Q price appreciation"
- "Sell 50% now, hold 50% for better prices"

**These recommendations assume the farmer HAS the option to hold.**

When the farmer has a health shock in Month 1:
- They sell EVERYTHING at whatever price
- The storage recommendation was irrelevant
- The price forecast was irrelevant
- The engine's sophisticated analysis was useless

---

## What the Product Needs Instead

### Module: Household Vulnerability Scanner

Before making ANY financial recommendation, assess:

| Vulnerability | Question | If High Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Health shock risk | Any household member with chronic illness? Recent hospitalization? | Suppress storage recommendations |
| Cash reserves | Savings equivalent to X months of expenses? | Prioritize liquidity recommendations |
| Credit access | Can you get a formal loan if needed? | Don't assume credit is available |
| Upcoming expenses | Any known large expenses (school fees, wedding, medical)? | Include in cash flow planning |

### Financial Product Integration

The Income Engine must be INTEGRATED with:
- Health insurance (Ayushman Bharat, state schemes)
- Emergency credit (small-ticket, fast-disbursement, not from moneylender)
- Savings products (weather-linked savings, harvest-linked deposits)

**Without these, the Income Engine is giving financial advice to people with no financial cushion.**

---

## The Correct Framing

**The income problem is not a market timing problem. It's a resilience problem.**

The platform should optimize for:
1. **Resilience first**: Does the farmer have emergency buffers?
2. **Liquidity second**: Can they meet upcoming obligations without distress sales?
3. **Then optimization**: If resilient and liquid, THEN consider storage/market timing

**Current framing**: "How to maximize income through better market timing"
**Correct framing**: "How to prevent catastrophic income loss through resilience"
